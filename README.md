# Household Energy Generation System

A hardware project that demonstrates how rotational motion from everyday household appliances can be converted into electrical energy and used to power small loads like lights.

---

## Project Overview

This project simulates a household energy system where a rotating device (modeled using a DC motor as a fan) generates electrical energy. This generated energy is then used to power LEDs representing household lighting.

The goal is to show how everyday motion in homes can be utilized to reduce dependency on external electricity and improve energy efficiency.

---

## Objectives

- Convert rotational motion into electrical energy  
- Demonstrate energy generation using simple components  
- Simulate a household system powering lights  
- Promote energy efficiency and sustainability  

---

## Working Principle

1. A DC motor acts as a fan (input source) and rotates a wheel  
2. This wheel is in contact with another wheel attached to a second motor  
3. The second motor acts as a generator  
4. As it rotates, it produces electrical energy  
5. This energy powers LEDs representing household loads  
6. A potentiometer controls motor speed  
7. An LCD displays system status and generated voltage  

---

## Key Observation

Even after switching OFF the power, the LED remains ON for a few seconds.

**Reason:**
- The rotating wheel stores kinetic energy  
- Due to inertia, it continues spinning briefly  
- This drives the generator motor  
- The generator continues producing electricity  

This demonstrates efficient utilization of leftover motion energy.
